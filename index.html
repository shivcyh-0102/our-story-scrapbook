About Us

A story that started quietly and became unforgettable.

Our story began unexpectedly way back in Grade 4. I still remember the first time I saw her, sitting in front of the class, writing on the board from her seat. In that quiet moment, something about her caught my attention. I wanted to talk to her, but I was too shy, so I admired her from a distance.

By Grade 6, we started walking home at the same time because one of my close friends was also friends with her. It was never planned, but we often ended up walking together. After elementary, we went our separate ways, and the feelings faded for a while.

High school came, then the pandemic. By chance, we became classmates again in Grade 7 and 8, though she did not remember me at first. Later on, we started talking more. She would message me, joke around, tell stories, and ask if we could walk home together.

One day, I finally said yes. We went to school together, and in that simple moment, something beautiful began. Grade 10 came, we spent more time together, and I slowly realized I was already falling for her.

On December 20, 2023, she said yes. From a quiet seat back in Grade 4 to the bond we built, ours was a story we never saw coming, but one that will always hold a real place in memory.
