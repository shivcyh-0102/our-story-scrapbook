For Marla

She’s Marla. She’s beautiful, cute, and for me, someone who will always be remembered with softness.

We had different interests, different ways of thinking, and there were times when we did not understand each other easily. But even through those hard moments, what we had still became a meaningful part of my life.

I’m proud of her because she’s strong. She has been through a lot, yet she keeps going. She carried pain, pressure, and problems that not everyone could see, but she still stood through them.

I know I was not perfect either. I had flaws, made mistakes, and had moments I wish I handled better. Still, this page keeps the honest part of the story: the care, the effort, and the memories that mattered.

She became my best friend, my safe space, my peace, and my person for an important season of my life. No matter where life goes from here, I’ll always be thankful for the chapter we shared.
